01

Onboarding

Seven steps, and the whole flow exists to earn one permission. The mailbox scope is the biggest ask this product will ever make, so it is explained before it is requested, in plain words, with what Loop cannot do stated as prominently as what it can. Tap through it — the frame on the left is live.

Every application you have ever submitted left a trace in your inbox: a confirmation, an auto-reply, an invitation, a no. Loop reads those traces and keeps the pipeline for you.

An interview is booked or moved

The seven steps

The order matters: the explanation comes before the consent screen, and the confirmation of what was found comes before any statistic is shown. A user who has not yet seen Loop be right about their own history has no reason to trust a ratio it computes.

03

Failure states

An automated tracker fails silently by default — it just stops finding things, and you would not notice for a week. So every failure has to be loud in exactly one place, say when the data was last trustworthy, and state whether anything was lost. Three of these four self-heal; only one needs you.

F1 Access revoked · needs you
Loop stopped reading 4 days ago
Google access was revoked or expired on 26 July

Nothing has been lost. Your 14 applications and their whole history are intact — Loop simply has not seen anything new since Saturday, and it will catch up on every missed message the moment you reconnect.
